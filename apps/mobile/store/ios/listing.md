**Description:**

[Name] helps you keep more of what you earn: see money in and out, set savings jars, plan the
next 30 days, and build an emergency fund, alone or with friendly challenges.

Private by design. Your transactions, statements and receipts stay on your device. We never
ask for a bank password, and there is no bank connection at all in this version. The optional
encrypted backup is locked with keys only you hold; we cannot read it.

- Got, Spent, Kept: your month in one line, with a plain verdict
- Money in & out: totals and categories from entries you add or statements you import (CSV,
  OFX, PDF), read entirely on your phone
- Jars and goals: save for something, pay something off, spend less, or build a habit, in one
  sentence
- Next 30 days: expected bills and paydays against your balance, with a shortfall warning
- Budget: 50/30/20 or zero-based, your pick
- Challenges: a monthly savings challenge with friends; only a percentage ever leaves your
  phone, never amounts
- Receipts: photograph a receipt; the photo stays on your phone with location data removed
- Sign-in with a passkey (Face ID); no passwords

Education, not advice: [Name] explains ideas in plain language and never tells you what to buy
or sell. Not a bank. Not financial advice.

Free to use. Private and Connected plans add more jars, unlimited statement accounts,
encrypted backup and household sharing; prices are shown in the app before any purchase, and
subscriptions renew until cancelled in your App Store settings.
